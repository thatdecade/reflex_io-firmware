Re:Flex Manual Calibration

Welcome to the Re:Flex Manual Calibration page. Here you can connect your device, view its sensor readings, and adjust sensor thresholds manually.

How It Works:

  1. Connect: Click the "Connect to HID Device" button to choose and connect your device.
  2. View Sensor Data: Once connected, the sensor readings and calibration controls will appear.
  3. Calibration Mode:
    • Status from device.
    • Must be enabled to save calibration.
    • Requires updated firmware.
  4. Manual Calibration:
    • Click the "Manual Calibration" button to show a slider.
    • Move the slider to change the sensor threshold value for that pad.
    • Requires updated firmware.

This simple interface lets you monitor your device and adjust its performance in real time.
